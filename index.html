Benefits of Filing ITR

A form in which taxpayers declare income details, exemptions, and deductions for a financial year.

🏠

Seamless Loan Processing

ITR copy is required when applying for car, home, and personal loans.

📄

Valid Income Proof

ITR is accepted as genuine income proof by every financial institution.

✈️

Easy Visa Processing

Required as proof of income in VISA applications for international travel.

💸

Claim TDS Refund

Through ITR filing, you can claim refund of TDS already deducted.

📉

Loss Compensation

ITR filing enables carrying forward of losses to subsequent financial years.

🚫

Avoid Penalties

Non-filing attracts a penalty of up to ₹10,000 — file on time to stay safe.

GST

GST Registration in India

Obtain your GSTIN — mandatory for eligible businesses.

Who Needs to Register?

  • Turnover above ₹40 lakh (goods) / ₹20 lakh (services)
  • Inter-state businesses
  • E-Commerce sellers
  • Reverse Charge Mechanism applicable businesses

Documents Required

  • PAN & Aadhaar Card
  • Business address proof
  • Bank account details

⚠️ Penalty for Non-Registration

  • 10% of tax due (minimum ₹10,000)
  • Higher penalty for tax evasion

Bank Loan

Documents Required for Bank Loan

We help you prepare and organise every document needed for smooth loan approval.

For Individuals (Personal / Business Loan)
  1. Aadhaar Card
  2. PAN Card
  3. Passport Size Photos
  4. Bank Statements (Last 6 Months)
  5. Income Proof (Salary Slips / ITR – 2–3 Years)
  6. Address Proof
  7. Existing Loan Details (if any)
For Business Loans (MSME / CC / OD / Term Loan)
  1. PAN Card (Individual & Firm)
  2. Aadhaar of Proprietor / Partners / Directors
  3. Business Registration Certificate
    • GST Certificate
    • Udyam Registration
    • Partnership Deed / MOA & AOA
  4. ITR (Last 2–3 Years)
  5. Financial Statements (P&L, Balance Sheet)

Business Registration

Types of Entities to Start a Business

We help you register the right business structure based on your goals.

👨‍💼

Proprietorship

Simplest form where an individual fully owns and controls the business. Ideal for solo entrepreneurs.

🤝

Partnership Firm

Owned by 2 or more people who share profits & losses. Best for professional collaborations.

🛡️

Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)

Combines benefits of a partnership with limited liability protection. Popular for service firms.

🧑‍💻

One Person Company (OPC)

Single individual operates a business with limited liability. Best for solo founders wanting corporate status.

🏢

Private Limited Company

A company owned by a small group with investor appeal. Preferred for scaling businesses.
